ABOUT

From Spatial Data to Software and Visual Business

I have run Mizumoto Photography since 2021, a visual services business covering weddings, pre-wedding sessions, and event documentation in Central Java. Leading it day to day has meant handling everything from client relationships to brand partnerships with local institutions.

Before that, I spent four years as a GIS and Remote Sensing Specialist. I worked on international watershed management and marine spatial planning projects, including a flood management study for the Citarum Hulu river basin funded by the Asian Development Bank, in collaboration with Deltares, PUSAIR, and ITB. That work is where precision and evidence-based analysis became second nature.

Now I am applying that same discipline to a new direction: building web applications and using AI tools to make business operations more efficient. It is not a career change so much as adding a new set of tools to the same way of working.
